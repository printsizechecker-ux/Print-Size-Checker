Why Use a Photo Border Tool for Printing?

Adding a white border (also known as a mat or margin) around your photos before printing is a professional technique used by photographers and galleries worldwide. Borders serve multiple purposes: they protect the image edges during handling, provide space for signatures or edition numbers, and create visual breathing room that makes the photograph stand out.

Exact Measurements Matter

Unlike basic online tools that use percentages, PrintSizeChecker's border tool lets you define borders in exact real-world units — inches, centimeters, millimeters, or pixels. This precision is essential when you need your print to fit a specific frame size or when your print lab requires exact dimensions.

Two Border Modes for Different Workflows

  • Inside Mode: The image shrinks to fit within your specified output dimensions, with borders applied inside. The final file size stays exactly as you specified — perfect when your frame or mat opening has fixed dimensions.
  • Outside Mode: The canvas expands around your image. The image itself stays at full resolution, and borders are added around it. The final file is larger than your specified dimensions — ideal when you want maximum image quality with a decorative border.

Image Fit with Uneven Borders

When your image aspect ratio doesn't match your print dimensions (for example, a 4:3 photo in an 8×10 frame), stretching the image distorts it and cropping loses parts of the photo. Our Image Fit option maintains the original aspect ratio and automatically calculates uneven borders to fill the remaining space — giving you a perfectly framed look without distortion or cropping.

Professional Print Compatibility

Every file you download includes the correct DPI metadata (72, 150, 200, 300, or 600 DPI) that professional print labs require. Export as high-quality JPG with adjustable compression, or lossless PNG for maximum fidelity. The built-in quality indicator shows you the actual DPI of your output, so you'll know before you print whether your image has enough resolution for sharp results.

Common Print Sizes with Borders

  • 8×10" with 0.5" border: Image area becomes 7×9" — fits standard 8×10 frames perfectly
  • 11×14" with 0.75" border: Image area becomes 9.5×12.5" — popular for gallery presentations
  • A4 with 1cm border: Image area becomes 18.7×25.7cm — ideal for European frame standards
  • 16×20" with 1" border: Image area becomes 14×18" — classic fine art print format
